Jarvis

A personal AI assistant that Adam Weir built for himself.

Jarvis runs on Adam's own computer. It isn't a product, it isn't offered to anyone else, and there are no accounts to sign up for. Adam is its only user.

What it does with Google

With Adam's sign-in, Jarvis reads his Google Calendar and Gmail to answer his questions: what's on this week, what an email said, when he's free. It can also prepare a calendar event, a reminder, an email, or an inbox action like archiving. None of those happen until Adam reviews them and clicks to approve.

How Jarvis handles that data is in the privacy policy. The rules for using it are in the terms of service.
